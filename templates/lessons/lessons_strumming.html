03 · Strumming

Find your rhythm

Choose a comfortable chord, such as E minor. Count out loud and keep your hand moving evenly.

Learn the strokes

D ↓ = Downstroke: move from thick strings toward thin strings. U ↑ = Upstroke: move back toward the thick strings, brushing lightly.

In 4/4 time, count 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &. Each number is a beat (a quarter note). The & halfway between beats makes eighth notes. A dash means pass over the strings without striking them.

Keep it comfortable

  • Hold your pick with a relaxed grip and keep your wrist loose.
  • Start slowly, around 60 beats per minute on a metronome. Play one downstroke per click first.
  • Keep the same motion through silent gaps. Listen for even spacing between strokes.
  • Practice on one chord before changing chords. Increase the tempo only when the pattern feels steady.
